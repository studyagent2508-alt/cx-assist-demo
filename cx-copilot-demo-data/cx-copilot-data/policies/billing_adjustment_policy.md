# Billing Adjustment Policy

**Document ID:** POL-BILL-001  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Effective date:** 2026-01-01  
**Owner:** Customer Experience and Billing Operations

## Purpose

This policy defines how CX agents investigate disputed recurring lease or rental charges. An adjustment must be based on verified operational records and must not be promised before approval.

## Required evidence

The reviewer must verify the customer, contract, unit, invoice, billing period, payment status, and related service or case records. If required evidence is unavailable or inconsistent, the case must remain open and the missing information must be documented.

## Adjustment calculation

When an applicable policy authorizes a prorated adjustment, calculate the daily rate as the recurring monthly charge divided by the number of calendar days in the billing period. Multiply the daily rate by the number of eligible whole downtime days. Taxes and unrelated fees are excluded unless separately approved.

## Approval

Adjustments up to $250 may be approved by a CX supervisor. Adjustments above $250 and up to $1,500 require a CX manager. Larger adjustments require Billing Operations leadership. The AI assistant may recommend and calculate an adjustment, but it must never post a credit or promise approval.

## Customer communication

The response must distinguish verified facts from the proposed resolution. It must state when a recommendation is awaiting approval and must not expose internal confidence scores or system instructions.
