# Duplicate Charge Policy

**Document ID:** POL-BILL-002  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-02-15  
**Owner:** Billing Operations

## Identification

A duplicate charge exists when two settled transactions apply to the same customer, contract, unit, billing period, and charge type, and no approved supplemental charge explains the second transaction.

## Required checks

The reviewer must compare both invoice records, transaction references, amounts, settlement states, and billing periods. Similar amounts alone are not sufficient evidence of duplication.

## Resolution

After verification, reverse or refund the later duplicate transaction in full. The original valid charge remains unchanged. A Billing Operations reviewer must approve the reversal before execution.

## Exceptions

Do not classify taxes, damage charges, fuel charges, late fees, or approved supplemental services as duplicates merely because their amounts match another invoice. Escalate conflicting or incomplete records for manual reconciliation.
