# CX Escalation and Approval Policy

**Document ID:** POL-CX-004  
**Version:** 1.3  
**Effective date:** 2026-04-01  
**Owner:** Customer Experience

## Escalation triggers

Escalate a case when it has multiple unresolved contacts, conflicting source-system records, a requested adjustment beyond the agent's authority, a safety concern, or a material service failure.

## Priority guidance

Safety concerns and active roadside incidents receive urgent priority. Duplicate financial transactions and service failures with repeated contacts receive high priority. General explanations without financial or operational impact normally receive standard priority.

## Human review

AI-generated summaries, recommendations, and response drafts are decision support only. A human reviewer must approve any credit, refund, contract change, escalation closure, or customer-facing response.

## Audit requirements

The case record must retain the evidence sources reviewed, applicable policy identifiers, recommended action, reviewer decision, edits to the proposed response, and timestamps.
