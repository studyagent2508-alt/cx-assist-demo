# Maintenance Downtime Credit Policy

**Document ID:** POL-SVC-003  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective date:** 2026-03-01  
**Owner:** Fleet Maintenance and Customer Experience

## Eligibility

A recurring-charge adjustment may be considered when an active covered unit is unavailable for more than 24 consecutive hours due to a verified unscheduled repair. Scheduled preventive maintenance, inspections, customer-caused damage, and downtime caused by customer delay are not automatically eligible.

## Eligible period

Eligibility begins after the first 24 consecutive hours of verified downtime. Only whole calendar days after that threshold are used in the standard prorated calculation. The service record must show opened and completed timestamps.

## Decision requirements

The reviewer must confirm that the service event overlaps the disputed invoice period and that the contract was active. If the cause or timestamps are disputed, refer the case to Fleet Maintenance before recommending a credit.

## Approval and communication

Any calculated credit follows the Billing Adjustment Policy approval thresholds. CX agents may explain that the case is under review but may not guarantee a credit before approval.
